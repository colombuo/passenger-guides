# Alder Junction

Alder Junction is the interchange between the Crossline and the riverside shuttle. Crossline trains use platforms 1 and 2; shuttle services use platform 3.

The main entrance faces Market Lane. For a step-free change to the shuttle, follow the signed passage beyond the ticket barriers rather than the footbridge. Allow a few extra minutes at the evening peak when the passage is shared with arriving passengers.

There are ticket machines beside the Market Lane entrance and a staffed help point on platform 2.
