# Dale Quay

import {TravelNote} from './_travel-note.mjs'

Dale Quay serves the ferry terminal and the north end of the waterfront. From the station, the signed walking route to the ferry check-in takes about seven minutes and crosses one signal-controlled road.

The waterfront exit has a lift between the concourse and platform level. The step-free route to the ferry uses the eastern pavement; the stairs opposite the exit are a shorter route only if steps are suitable.

<TravelNote>Ferry departures are operated separately from Moe Rails. Check the ferry operator's boarding time before making a tight connection.</TravelNote>
