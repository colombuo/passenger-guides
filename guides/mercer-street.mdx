# Mercer Street

Mercer Street serves the university quarter. The main exit is on the west side of the road, next to the public library. The east exit is open during station hours but has steps.

For a step-free journey to the campus, use the west exit and the crossing at Library Walk. Continue past the library return to the campus west gate; the walk takes about four minutes. The direct alley to the east gate has a steep gradient and is not the signed accessible route.

There is a sheltered waiting area on platform 2. The station has no public toilets.

The campus shuttle stops on Library Walk, beyond the signal-controlled crossing. It does not call at the east-side bus stop beside the steps.
