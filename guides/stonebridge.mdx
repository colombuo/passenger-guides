# Stonebridge

import {TravelNote} from './_travel-note.mjs'

Stonebridge station has entrances on Bridge Street and Canal Walk. The Canal Walk entrance is nearest the towpath, but it closes when the riverside flood barrier is in use.

Platforms 1 and 2 are connected by lifts and the central bridge. For the community clinic, leave by Bridge Street and follow the pavement past the post office.

<TravelNote>During events at the sports ground, station staff may direct passengers through a one-way queue on the concourse. Follow the signs in place on the day.</TravelNote>
