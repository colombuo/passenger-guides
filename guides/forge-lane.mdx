# Forge Lane

Forge Lane has two side platforms. Northbound trains leave from platform 1, beside the ticket machines. Southbound trains leave from platform 2 across the footbridge.

The accessible route between platforms is via the Forge Lane road crossing and the second entrance on Mill Street. It is longer than the footbridge, so passengers changing direction should allow at least ten minutes.

Cycle parking is outside both entrances. Cycles must not be left against the ramp handrails.
