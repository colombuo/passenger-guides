# Harbour Market

Harbour Market is busiest on Friday afternoons and during the Sunday market. Both entrances lead to the same concourse, but the north entrance is closer to the covered market.

The lifts connect the concourse with platforms 1 and 2. A wide gate is next to the staffed ticket window. When the window is closed, use the help point beside the gate for assistance.

The bus interchange is a three-minute walk from the south entrance. The stop for the hospital service is on the far side of Market Square.
