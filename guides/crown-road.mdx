# Crown Road

The Crown Road entrance opens onto the south end of platform 1. Platform 2 is reached by the subway under the tracks; the ramp is at its far end.

The short-stay pick-up bay is on Crown Road, opposite the pharmacy. Drivers should not stop at the bus stand outside the station forecourt. The stop for the town-centre bus is beyond the crossing, not beside the pick-up bay.

Last services are listed on the departure boards. The ticket office closes earlier than the station, but the machines remain available while trains are running.

The subway ramp has handrails on both sides. If the ramp is temporarily closed, ask station staff before changing platforms; there is no step-free route through the road bridge.
